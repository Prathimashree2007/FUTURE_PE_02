# Platform Adaptation Prompt

Adapt the FitFlex Adjustable Dumbbells UGC content for three short-form platforms:

1. Instagram Reels
2. Instagram Ads
3. YouTube Shorts

For each platform, provide:

- Recommended video length
- Opening style
- Pacing
- Visual suggestions
- Tone
- CTA placement

Keep the content conversational and suitable for short-form video.

The final content should feel like a real person sharing their experience rather than a traditional polished advertisement.
