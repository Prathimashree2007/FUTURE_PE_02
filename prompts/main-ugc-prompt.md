# UGC Ad Script Generator

Create authentic UGC-style advertising content for the following product.

## Product

Product Name: FitFlex Adjustable Dumbbells

Product Type: D2C Fitness Product

Target Audience: College students, working professionals, beginners and people who prefer working out at home.

Main Problem: Traditional dumbbells and gym equipment can take up too much space and may be inconvenient for people setting up a home workout area.

Solution / USP: Adjustable dumbbells provide different weight options in one compact setup, making home workouts easier to organize.

Target Platforms: Instagram Reels, Instagram Ads and YouTube Shorts.

## Instructions

Generate:

### 1. Hooks

Create 5 different hooks that:

- Grab attention quickly.
- Relate to the target audience's problem.
- Sound like something a real person might say.
- Avoid sounding like a traditional advertisement.
- Use simple and conversational language.

### 2. UGC Scripts

Create 3 short UGC-style video scripts.

Each script should follow:

HOOK → PROBLEM → SOLUTION → BENEFIT → CTA

The scripts should:

- Sound natural and conversational.
- Feel like a personal recommendation.
- Use short and simple sentences.
- Include realistic product benefits.
- Avoid exaggerated claims.
- Be suitable for approximately 15–60 seconds.

### 3. CTAs

Generate 8 short and natural calls to action.

### 4. Captions

Generate 2 Instagram captions suitable for the product.

### 5. Platform Adaptation

Explain how the content can be adapted for:

- Instagram Reels
- Instagram Ads
- YouTube Shorts

## Authenticity Guidelines

The scripts should feel like genuine UGC rather than a highly polished commercial.

Use:

- First-person language
- Conversational sentences
- Natural reactions
- Relatable situations
- Simple vocabulary

Avoid:

- Fake statistics
- Unsupported claims
- Overly dramatic language
- Excessive marketing language
- Unrealistic results

## Output

Organize the result into:

1. Product Overview
2. Target Audience
3. Core Problem
4. Solution / USP
5. Hooks
6. UGC Scripts
7. CTAs
8. Captions
9. Platform Adaptation
10. Authenticity Notes
